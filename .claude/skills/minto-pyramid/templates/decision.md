# Decision template

1. **Recommendation** — one preferred option and why it wins on the decisive criteria.
2. **Decision criteria** — only the criteria that materially change the choice.
3. **Options** — 2–4 realistic options at the same logical level.
4. **Trade-offs** — evidence-backed advantages, disadvantages, uncertainty, and reversibility.
5. **Risks and mitigations** — only material risks.
6. **Next action** — owner/action/timeframe when requested or operationally necessary.

The recommendation must synthesize the comparison; do not leave the user to infer the decision from a feature list.
