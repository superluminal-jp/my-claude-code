# Completion check

Before returning a substantive result, verify the observable output:

1. What single question does this answer?
2. Is the governing answer clear early enough for the requested format?
3. Does every first-level point directly support that answer?
4. Are sibling points logically comparable and at a similar abstraction level?
5. Does each sibling group use one coherent ordering principle?
6. Does each parent synthesize rather than merely label its children?
7. Are evidence and detail below the claims they support?
8. Are assumptions and uncertainty explicit where material?
9. Are irrelevant points removed or relocated?
10. Does the result comply with the user's requested format and scope?

If a criterion is not applicable, skip it rather than inventing structure.
