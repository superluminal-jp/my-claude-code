# Genres

Where the answer goes (first-read location):

| medium | apex |
|---|---|
| paper | title, abstract, end of introduction |
| report, proposal | title, executive summary, end of introduction |
| email, chat | subject, first 1 to 2 sentences |
| slides | opening or summary slide; every slide title is a claim |
| talk | first minute |

Typical key lines (defaults, adjust to local practice):

| genre | key line | order |
|---|---|---|
| research paper | valid design, results, conclusion | deductive (skeptical reader) |
| proposal, decision doc | reasons for the recommendation (benefit, cost, risk) | comparative (by importance) |
| incident report, postmortem | cause, prevention | structural (split of the question); history goes to the intro |
| procedure, manual | steps | chronological |
| survey, comparison | per option, or per criterion | structural or comparative |
| minutes, status email | decisions, open items, next actions | structural |

## Papers (IMRaD)
- ICMJE says original research is "usually" divided into IMRaD, so check the target journal before reordering.
- Read IMRaD as a deductive key line: Methods (design is valid, major premise), Results (minor premise), Discussion (conclusion within scope).
- Introduction: situation, complication, question (Swales' CARS move 3 may announce the main findings), then the answer.
- Number corresponding subsections (M1 with R1); check with `--pair M:R`.
- Reproduction details are records: supplement or end of subsection (ICMJE asks Methods to allow reproduction; use CONSORT 2025 for RCTs).
- Discussion keeps interpretation, rejected alternatives and limits; significance moves to the abstract and the end of the introduction.

Sources: Minto, The Minto Pyramid Principle (1987/2009); Mensh & Kording 2017, PLoS Comput Biol 13(9):e1005619; ICMJE Recommendations; Hopewell et al. 2025, BMJ 389:e081123; Swales 1990; Gopen & Swan 1990, Am Sci 78(6):550-558.
